## Verification protocol (mandatory — do this for every job before it goes in the email)
- Recency bias: strongly prefer roles with a visible posted date within the last ~2 weeks. Treat "posted 3+ weeks ago" or "posted date unknown" as a yellow flag, not neutral — it needs extra scrutiny below, not a pass.
- Aggregator sites (Glassdoor, LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Lensa, Jobright, hercjobs, insidehighered, jobleads, etc.) are LEADS ONLY, never confirmation. They cache stale postings for weeks. Finding the same listing on three of them is not three independent confirmations — it's one stale copy, scraped three times.
- Real confirmation means one of:
  (a) Directly loading the employer's own official careers/jobs page (not a third-party mirror) and seeing the exact title still listed today, or
  (b) Directly loading the original posting URL itself (employer's ATS/Workday/iCIMS/etc.) and confirming it doesn't show closed/filled/expired.
  If WebFetch is blocked (403) on both the org's careers page and the original posting, that job is UNVERIFIED — see next rule.
- No caveats, no guessing: if a job's current open status can't be confirmed with real confidence after genuine effort, DROP it. Do not include it "just in case" or hedge in the email. Sending 1-2 solidly verified jobs beats sending 3 where one might be dead — repeated stale sends cost more trust than a short email.
- Before finalizing the email, do one last pass: for each of the 3 picks, be able to state in your own head *how* you confirmed it's live today (which page, what you saw) — not just that it appeared in a search result.
